Adopt means use it. Trial means try it. Assess means watch it. Hold means avoid for now. Each tool links the proof.

What the rings mean

  • Adopt. Use it. Stable, updated in the last 6 months, lots of people use it.
  • Trial. Try it. Still young (before 1.0), or quiet for 6 to 18 months, or few users yet.
  • Assess. Watch it. Brand new, less than a year old.
  • Hold. Avoid for now. Dead, deprecated, or no update in 18 months.

Dot shape shows the license: ● open source, ■ free or free tier, ◆ paid. Every tool links the release page or notice that puts it in its ring.
