How to get your website cited by ChatGPT and Perplexity

The practical checklist for being found and quoted by AI answers: let crawlers in, serve readable HTML, state facts plainly and make each page worth citing.

To get cited, make sure AI crawlers can fetch your pages, serve the content as plain HTML, and write pages that answer one question clearly with facts a model can quote. No trick forces a citation, so the goal is to remove every reason you would be skipped.

Step 1: Let the crawlers in

Many sites block AI bots by accident, through robots.txt rules, a firewall or a bot-protection page. If the bot gets a block page, it cannot cite you. Fetch your page the way a bot does and look at the status code and body.

curl -sI -A 'GPTBot' https://example.com/ | head -n 1
curl -sI -A 'PerplexityBot' https://example.com/ | head -n 1

You want 200 for both. A 403 or a challenge page means a firewall rule is in the way. Details are in robots.txt for AI crawlers.

Step 2: Put the content in the HTML

Several AI crawlers do not run JavaScript, so a page that builds its text in the browser can look empty to them. Open view-source and check that your main text is there. See whether AI search sees JavaScript pages.

Step 3: Write pages that answer a question

Give each page one topic. Put the direct answer in the first two sentences, then the detail. Use a descriptive title and headings that match how people ask. State facts with numbers, names and dates where they matter, since vague claims are not worth quoting. More in writing content AI answers quote.

Step 4: Make the page easy to identify

Add a clear title, a meta description, a canonical URL and a sitemap. Add structured data where it fits, see JSON-LD for AI search. These help both classic search and the retrieval systems behind AI answers.

Step 5: Check, then measure

Run a checker rather than guessing. Citable checks whether AI search and chatbots can find, read and cite a website, and it points to what is blocking them. Afterwards, watch your logs for AI user agents and referrals, as described in tracking AI search traffic.

What not to expect

Being crawlable does not guarantee a citation. Models and search products choose sources by their own rules, which change. Keep the work grounded in things that are good for readers anyway: fast, readable, accurate pages.

Revisit the list every quarter. Products change what they fetch and how they pick sources, so a page that was fine last year may need a new look, and a small monthly check is cheaper than a big rescue later.

Questions people ask

Is there a way to force ChatGPT to cite my site?

No. You can only remove blockers and make the page a good source. Whether a page is cited is decided by the product.

Does classic SEO still matter?

Yes. AI search products often use search indexes and the open web, so crawlable, well-structured pages help in both.
