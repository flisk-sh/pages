How to track AI search traffic and citations

Find out whether AI products visit and send people to your site: read user agents in logs, spot referrals from assistants, and test your own questions by hand.

Check your server logs for AI crawler user agents, look in your analytics for referrals from assistant domains, and ask the assistants your own target questions on a schedule. No single dashboard shows all citations, so combine these three signals.

1. Count AI crawler visits in the logs

Crawler visits show that your pages are reachable. Search your access log for the known bot names.

grep -E 'GPTBot|OAI-SearchBot|ChatGPT-User|ClaudeBot|PerplexityBot' access.log \
  | awk '{print $9}' | sort | uniq -c

The field number depends on your log format. The output counts responses by status code. Mostly 200 is healthy. Many 403 or 429 means a firewall is blocking them. If you are behind a CDN, use the CDN's analytics, since the origin may not see all requests.

2. Look for referrals

When a person clicks a link inside an assistant, the visit may arrive with a referrer such as chatgpt.com or perplexity.ai, and some products add a utm parameter. In your analytics, filter referrers by those domains. Names and behaviour change, so treat this as an approximate count, since many clicks arrive with no referrer at all.

3. Test your own questions

Write a list of 10 to 20 questions your customers ask, such as "best open-source tool for X". Ask each assistant once a month and record whether your site is cited, who is cited instead, and what the answer says about you. Keep the results in a spreadsheet or a file in git.

date,assistant,question,cited,competitors
2026-11-01,assistantA,"best open-source X",no,"ToolB;ToolC"

Answers vary between runs, so look at trends over months, not single results.

4. Fix what the tracking shows

  • Bots blocked: see robots.txt for AI crawlers.
  • Bots reach you but you are never cited: improve the page, see writing content AI answers quote.
  • A competitor is cited with a clear comparison page: write an honest one of your own.

5. Add a repeatable check

Run Citable on your key pages after each release to catch a regression in crawlability. Keep expectations modest: AI answers are not deterministic, so use tracking to catch breakage and to find content gaps, not to chase a precise number. For the basic checklist, see how to get cited by ChatGPT and Perplexity.

Write down what you will do with the numbers before you collect them. For example: if bot hits return errors, fix the firewall that week; if a key question never cites you, rewrite that page that month.

Questions people ask

Can I see exactly how often I am cited?

Not with one tool. Combine log data, referral data and manual question tests, and treat each as a partial view.

Why do I see crawler hits but no traffic?

Crawling and citing are separate. Being fetched does not mean being linked. Improve how directly and clearly the page answers the question.
