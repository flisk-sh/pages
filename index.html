How your portfolio works

Rate only what you have really used: 1 used it, 2 use it often, 3 expert. Your best-fit role comes from the 44 job loadouts on the Roles tab: a must-have tool counts 3, an every-day tool 2, a good-to-know tool 1, each times your level. Your skill shape adds up your ratings per family of tools.

Nothing is sent anywhere. Your ratings live in your browser and in your page link, so the link is your page. Download it as a single web page to host it yourself.
