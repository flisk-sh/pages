How GPUs work

A CPU has a few very fast cores that each do one thing at a time. A GPU has thousands of slow ones doing the same thing to different data, all at once. Paint the same picture with both and watch.

Where it does not

When each step needs the answer of the one before (a chain of if-this-then-that), thousands of cores just wait in line. That is still a CPU's job. Graphics, matrix maths and neural networks are the opposite: millions of small, independent sums.
