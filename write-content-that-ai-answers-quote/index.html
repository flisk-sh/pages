How to write content that AI answers quote

Write pages that models can lift cleanly: a direct answer first, one topic per page, concrete facts, clear headings and sources that make the claim checkable.

  2. Make passages self-contained
  3. Be specific and checkable

Answer the question in the first two sentences of the page, keep to one topic, and back claims with specific facts. AI answers tend to quote passages that are direct, self-contained and easy to verify, which is also what readers want.

Lead with the answer

Put the conclusion before the background. If the title is a question, the first paragraph should answer it in plain words. A reader or a model that stops after two sentences should already have the answer.

Set the base URL to http://localhost:11434/v1, pass any non-empty API key, and use an Ollama model name.

Make passages self-contained

A model may lift one paragraph without the rest. Avoid "as mentioned above" and "this" with no noun. Name the thing in each paragraph: say "the Acme CLI" rather than "it". Short paragraphs with one idea each are easiest to reuse.

Be specific and checkable

  • Use exact commands, numbers, versions and dates.
  • Name the source of a claim and link to it.
  • State limits and trade-offs. Balanced pages read as more trustworthy than pure promotion.
  • Avoid hype words. They add nothing a model can quote.

Use headings that match questions

Headings such as "How to install", "Requirements" and "Limits" tell a parser what each block holds. Add a short FAQ with real questions people ask, with answers of one or two sentences. Mark it with FAQPage data as in the JSON-LD guide, and keep the visible text identical to the markup.

Keep pages current and owned

Show the date a page was last reviewed only if you really review it. Remove or redirect pages that are out of date. State who runs the site on an About page and keep a clear product name across your site, GitHub and social profiles, so a model can connect them. Publish original data or examples when you can, since a page that only repeats other pages gives a model no reason to cite it.

A before and after

Weak: "Our platform offers a powerful way to handle many deployment needs." Better: "Run flisk up to start every service in a project on your laptop. It needs Docker for container services." The second version names a command, a result and a requirement. A model can quote it, a reader can act on it, and you can check that it is true. Rewrite your top five pages this way before you touch anything technical.

Questions people ask

Should I write for models or humans?

Write for humans in a clear, direct style. Pages that are easy for a person to skim are also easy for a model to quote.

How long should a page be?

As long as the topic needs. One focused topic per page matters more than word count.
